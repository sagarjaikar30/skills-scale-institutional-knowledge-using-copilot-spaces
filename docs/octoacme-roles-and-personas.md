# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization by identifying security controls, compliance obligations, and risk mitigations required for project delivery. They help teams ship safely without creating avoidable governance or regulatory issues.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Define required controls, approvals, and review checkpoints
- Partner with engineering and PMs to mitigate identified risks
- Support incident readiness, audit preparation, and access controls
- Escalate unresolved security concerns through governance and escalation paths

### Goals
- Reduce product and operational risk
- Maintain compliance with policy and regulatory expectations
- Build secure-by-default delivery practices

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and architecture checkpoints
- Risk register updates and escalation notes
- Policy guidance and approval workflows

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to ensure security gates are included in plans and milestones.
- Collaborates with Developers to review design decisions, dependencies, and remediation work.
- Aligns with Product Managers to ensure customer and business requirements do not conflict with compliance needs.
- Provides early risk visibility to stakeholders so business decisions can account for security trade-offs.

---

## QA / Release Manager

### Role Summary
QA and Release Managers ensure the team ships high-quality, well-tested changes that are ready for production. They define readiness checks, coordinate validation activities, and support reliable releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, acceptance validation, and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate quality checks across functional, integration, and smoke testing
- Track defects, release blockers, and validation outcomes
- Partner with Developers and Project Managers on launch timing and rollback planning
- Own the final readiness assessment before deployment

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and reduce production defects
- Keep quality work visible and measurable throughout the lifecycle
- Maintain a predictable and low-risk deployment process

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and QA signoff updates
- Risk and defect triage in planning and review meetings
- Release notes and stakeholder-ready status summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure test coverage, defects, and validation evidence align with acceptance criteria.
- Provides Project Managers with release readiness information and deployment risk signals.
- Supports Product Managers by confirming that quality and business expectations are met before launch.
- Coordinates with stakeholders on launch windows, rollback readiness, and post-release verification.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads track product performance, adoption, and operational health so teams can make decisions using evidence instead of assumptions. They connect product outcomes to measurable business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation requirements
- Monitor adoption, conversion, latency, error, and quality signals
- Partner with PM and Product teams to interpret data trends and customer impact
- Validate whether releases achieved intended outcomes
- Surface risks or opportunities that warrant follow-up action

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with measurable evidence
- Connect delivery work to customer and business outcomes
- Identify product improvements based on observed trends

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards and weekly performance summaries
- Metrics reviews during planning and stakeholder updates
- Evidence-based recommendations for prioritization and iteration

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define the metrics that indicate success or failure.
- Provides Project Managers with delivery health indicators and milestone risk signals.
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure the right telemetry and data collection is in place.
- Helps stakeholders understand the impact of changes through clear reporting and trend analysis.

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Liaisons connect delivery teams with customer expectations, business needs, and stakeholder concerns. They help translate feedback into priorities and keep communication aligned across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder feedback and surface customer pain points
- Coordinate communication around milestones, trade-offs, and release impacts
- Represent the voice of customers and internal stakeholders in planning discussions
- Support adoption and change management for new features or process updates
- Escalate unresolved stakeholder concerns to the appropriate decision makers

### Goals
- Improve customer and stakeholder confidence in delivery outcomes
- Align project execution with real-world customer needs
- Maintain healthy communication and expectation management

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone communications
- Customer feedback reviews and advisory discussions
- Change readiness and launch communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Brings customer and stakeholder context to Product Managers and Project Managers during prioritization and planning.
- Works with Developers to clarify user scenarios and validate readiness against real-world use cases.
- Helps Project Managers coordinate messaging, approvals, and stakeholder alignment.
- Provides Product Managers with insights that influence roadmap decisions and success measures.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intentionally complementary: they clarify ownership, strengthen accountability, and help the team deliver with better alignment across product, engineering, quality, risk, and stakeholder communication.
